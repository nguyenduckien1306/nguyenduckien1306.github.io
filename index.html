📊 What Is This Tool?

FortyGuard Heat Intelligence is a smart city tool that helps you understand, track, and act on heat patterns in urban areas. It takes the guesswork out of temperature monitoring and gives you clear, easy-to-read heat maps, automatic warnings, and a simple way to decide where action is needed most.

This application is perfect for city planners, facility managers, weather watchers, sustainability teams, and anyone who needs to know how heat affects their surroundings. It processes large amounts of thermal information and presents it visually so you can understand quickly and act confidently.

✨ Key Features Made Easy

🗺️ Interactive Heat Maps

Explore your area with a beautiful, colour-coded view of temperature patterns. Zoom in on any street, park, or building zone. The map responds smoothly - you can pan and click to see numbers.

🚨 Automatic Alert System

Set your own threshold limit. When certain zones break the temperature threshold, the system automatically highlights them. You don't need to watch all the time - it'll tell you.

🎯 Priority Scoring

Don't know where to start? The platform scores each area based on heat risk and impact. This rank is shown with a simple number - higher number means more urgent attention.

🔍 Investigation Queue

When something seems off, this tool creates a checklist or 'queue' for you to examine. You can name possible causes, assign work, and keep track of what you've checked.

🔏 Cryptographically Secure Logging

Every data point you save is locked with secure certificate-style protection. If you need to share your data or reports, this ensures the evidence hasn't been tampered with.

🧠 Explainable Results

You'll see not just what's hot, but why it's flagged as hot. The platform shows the indicators and tells you in plain language what influences your data.

🖥️ Meet Your Screen Layout

When you open the application, you'll see a clean interface with a large map at the center. On the right side, a control panel lets you adjust time, temperature scale, and alerts. At the bottom, you have an inbox - this is your "Investigation Queue". It lists locations that need looking at.

📈 Using the Map

  1. Click anywhere on the map to see that spot's temperature reading and score.
  2. Use the + and - buttons (or your mouse wheel) to zoom closer or out.
  3. Look at the colour legend - usually at the top right of the map - to understand what each colour means.

🔔 Managing Alerts

  1. Click the ⚙️ Settings icon in the top bar.
  2. Find the "Heat Alert Threshold" box. Type the temperature that you want to be warned about, like 35°C or 90°F.
  3. Click Save. From now on, any zone that crosses this line will immediately pop up in your notification tray.
